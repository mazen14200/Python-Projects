# English Grammar Advanced Mastery — Egyptian Arabic

> شرح عميق بالمصري، مع أمثلة إنجليزية طبيعية وترجمة مصرية. الهدف إنك تفهم **المعنى قبل اسم القاعدة**.

# 1. Question Word + Infinitive

## الشكل
`what / where / when / how / which / who + to + base verb`

بنستخدمه لما نختصر فكرة زي: "أعمل إيه؟ أروح فين؟ أبدأ إزاي؟"

### أهم 5 أمثلة
1. **I don't know what to do.** — مش عارف أعمل إيه.
2. **Can you tell me how to use this?** — ممكن تقولي أستخدم ده إزاي؟
3. **I'm not sure where to go.** — مش متأكد أروح فين.
4. **We need to decide when to leave.** — لازم نقرر نتحرك إمتى.
5. **She couldn't decide which dress to buy.** — مقدرتش تقرر تشتري أنهي فستان.

### الفكرة العميقة
**What should I do?** = سؤال مباشر.  
**I don't know what I should do.** = سؤال غير مباشر.  
**I don't know what to do.** = اختصار طبيعي جدًا.

غالبًا الشخص اللي هينفذ الفعل مفهوم من السياق.

**who to ask / what to say / where to go / how to fix it** كلها شائعة.

> `why + infinitive` مش هو النمط المعتاد: بدل **I don't know why to go** نقول **I don't know why I should go.**

# 2. Modal Perfect

## الشكل
`modal + have + V3`

الـ Modal Perfect بيتكلم عن **الماضي**، لكن بيضيف موقف المتكلم: استنتاج، احتمال، لوم، ندم، فرصة ضاعت، أو نتيجة افتراضية.

## must have + V3
= استنتاج قوي عن الماضي.

1. **He must have forgotten the meeting.** — أكيد تقريبًا نسي الاجتماع.
2. **She must have been tired.** — أكيد كانت تعبانة.
3. **They must have missed the train.** — أكيد فاتهم القطر.
4. **You must have heard the news.** — أكيد سمعت الخبر.
5. **He must have left early.** — أكيد مشي بدري.

**must be tired** = غالبًا تعبان دلوقتي.  
**must have been tired** = غالبًا كان تعبان وقتها.

## can't have + V3
= استنتاج قوي إن حاجة ماحصلتش / مستحيل تكون حصلت.

1. **He can't have forgotten.**
2. **She can't have said that.**
3. **They can't have finished already.**
4. **He can't have known about it.**
5. **You can't have seen him yesterday.**

## may/might have + V3
= احتمال في الماضي.

1. **He may have forgotten.**
2. **She might have missed the train.**
3. **They may have taken the wrong road.**
4. **He might have misunderstood you.**
5. **She may have left early.**

الفرق:
- **must have** = شبه متأكد.
- **may/might have** = احتمال.
- **can't have** = شبه متأكد إن ده مستحيل.

## could have + V3
إما احتمال أو فرصة كانت متاحة لكنها لم تحصل.

1. **You could have called me.** — كان ممكن تتصل بيا.
2. **We could have won the game.**
3. **She could have been seriously injured.**
4. **He could have asked for help.**
5. **I could have helped you.**

## should have + V3
= كان المفروض يحصل لكنه ماحصلش.

1. **You should have told me.**
2. **I should have studied harder.**
3. **We should have left earlier.**
4. **He should have apologized.**
5. **She should have checked the email.**

## shouldn't have + V3
= الحاجة حصلت، لكن مكنش المفروض تحصل.

1. **You shouldn't have said that.**
2. **I shouldn't have eaten so much.**
3. **He shouldn't have trusted him.**
4. **We shouldn't have waited so long.**
5. **She shouldn't have spent so much money.**

## would have + V3
غالبًا نتيجة شرط ماضي.

**If I had known, I would have helped you.**  
= لو كنت عرفت، كنت ساعدتك.

1. **I would have called you.**
2. **She would have helped us.**
3. **We would have won.**
4. **He would have come if you had invited him.**
5. **I would have told you if I had known.**

## needn't have + V3
الحاجة حصلت فعلًا، لكن مكنتش ضرورية.

**You needn't have brought any food.**  
= مكنش لازم تجيب أكل، بس أنت جبته فعلًا.

الفرق:
- **You didn't need to bring food.** = مكنش مطلوب؛ مش واضح إذا جبته ولا لأ.
- **You needn't have brought food.** = جبته فعلًا، لكنه مكنش ضروري.

# 3. Causative with Person

هنا شخص **بيخلي شخص تاني يعمل الفعل**.

## make + person + base verb
= يجبر/يخلي.

1. **She made me apologize.**
2. **The teacher made us stay after class.**
3. **My parents made me clean my room.**
4. **The manager made him explain the problem.**
5. **The movie made me cry.**

مهم:
**make me go** وليس **make me to go**.

لكن في Passive:
**I was made to apologize.**

## have + person + base verb
= يطلب/يرتب إن شخص يعمل حاجة، ومفيد جدًا في الشغل.

1. **I'll have John call you.**
2. **She had her assistant book the tickets.**
3. **The manager had me check the report.**
4. **I'll have someone fix the problem.**
5. **He had his team review the code.**

## get + person + to + verb
= يقدر يقنع/يخلي شخص يعمل حاجة.

1. **I got him to help me.**
2. **She got her son to clean his room.**
3. **We got the client to agree.**
4. **He got me to change my mind.**
5. **They got the team to work together.**

### احفظها كده
- **make + person + V**
- **have + person + V**
- **get + person + to V**

# 4. Future Time Clauses

بعد:
`when / before / after / until / as soon as / once`

لما بنتكلم عن المستقبل، غالبًا نستخدم **Present Simple** في جملة الزمن، وFuture في الجملة الأساسية.

❌ **When I will arrive, I'll call you.**  
✅ **When I arrive, I'll call you.**

### أشهر 5
1. **When I arrive, I'll call you.**
2. **I'll call you when I get home.**
3. **As soon as I finish, I'll let you know.**
4. **We'll start when everyone arrives.**
5. **I'll wait until you come back.**

أمثلة إضافية:
- **I'll call you before I leave.**
- **I'll call you after I finish work.**
- **Once I finish the report, I'll send it to you.**
- **I won't leave until you arrive.**
- **I won't go unless you come with me.**

الفكرة: **when** نفسها بتحدد وقت مستقبلي، فمش محتاجة **will** داخلها.

# 5. Speak vs Talk

## Speak
أكثر ارتباطًا باللغة، أو الكلام الرسمي، أو مخاطبة شخص/جمهور.

### أشهر 5
1. **Do you speak English?**
2. **Can I speak to the manager?**
3. **I need to speak to you.**
4. **She speaks three languages.**
5. **He spoke at the meeting.**

**speak English** طبيعي جدًا، وليس عادة **talk English**.

## Talk
أكثر شيوعًا للمحادثة والنقاش اليومي.

### أشهر 5
1. **We need to talk.**
2. **I talked to my friend yesterday.**
3. **Can I talk to you?**
4. **They were talking about work.**
5. **We talked for two hours.**

**I spoke to my manager** و **I talked to my manager** الاتنين صح. `talk to` يومية جدًا، و`speak to` قد تبدو أكثر رسمية حسب السياق.

# 6. Say vs Tell

## Say
التركيز على **الكلام نفسه**.

`say + something`

## Tell
التركيز على **الشخص الذي يستقبل الكلام**.

`tell + person + something`

❌ **He said me.**  
✅ **He told me.**  
أو: **He said it to me.**

### Say — أشهر 5
1. **She said she was tired.**
2. **He said he didn't know.**
3. **What did you say?**
4. **Don't say that.**
5. **She said goodbye.**

### Tell — أشهر 5
1. **Tell me the truth.**
2. **He told me everything.**
3. **She told him to wait.**
4. **Don't tell anyone.**
5. **I told you so.**

# 7. Have / Have Got

## Have
ممكن تعني:
- يمتلك
- عنده
- يتناول
- يعمل نشاط
- عنده مشكلة/تجربة

**I have a car.**  
**I have breakfast at 8.**  
**I have a problem.**

## Have got
غالبًا للملكية أو الحالة:

**I've got a car.**  
**I've got a problem.**

في الملكية:
**I have two brothers.** ≈ **I've got two brothers.**

السؤال:
- **Do you have a car?**
- **Have you got a car?**

النفي:
- **I don't have a car.**
- **I haven't got a car.**

### أشهر 5
1. **I've got a problem.**
2. **Do you have any questions?**
3. **I've got two brothers.**
4. **She has a new job.**
5. **Have you got a minute?**

# 8. Make vs Do

دي من أهم الـ collocations؛ الأفضل تحفظ التعبير كاملًا.

## Make
غالبًا إنتاج/إنشاء/نتيجة/قرار/تأثير.

### أشهر 5
1. **make a decision**
2. **make a mistake**
3. **make money**
4. **make a plan**
5. **make a choice**

إضافات مهمة:
**make a promise / make an appointment / make a suggestion / make a phone call / make progress / make an effort / make a difference / make a complaint / make a reservation / make a change**

## Do
غالبًا نشاط أو مهمة أو عمل.

### أشهر 5
1. **do homework**
2. **do the work**
3. **do your best**
4. **do business**
5. **do research**

إضافات مهمة:
**do the dishes / do the laundry / do the shopping / do a job / do a task / do something / do nothing / do well / do badly**

# 9. Question Tags

بنضيف سؤال صغير في النهاية للتأكيد أو طلب agreement.

### القاعدة
جملة مثبتة → tag منفي.

**You're tired, aren't you?**

جملة منفية → tag مثبت.

**You aren't tired, are you?**

### أشهر 5
1. **It's a nice day, isn't it?**
2. **You're coming, aren't you?**
3. **You don't like coffee, do you?**
4. **He works here, doesn't he?**
5. **They've finished, haven't they?**

حالات مهمة:
- **I'm late, aren't I?**
- **Let's go, shall we?**
- **Open the door, will you?**
- **Nobody called, did they?**

# 10. Reported Questions

بنستخدمها لما ننقل سؤال شخص بدل ما نكرر كلامه حرفيًا.

## Yes/No

Direct:
**"Are you ready?"**

Reported:
**He asked me if I was ready.**

## Wh-question

Direct:
**"Where do you live?"**

Reported:
**He asked me where I lived.**

### أهم قاعدة
بعد `asked + wh-word` نستخدم **ترتيب الجملة العادي**:

`subject + verb`

مش ترتيب السؤال.

❌ **He asked me where did I live.**  
✅ **He asked me where I lived.**

### أشهر 5
1. **She asked me if I was ready.**
2. **He asked where I lived.**
3. **They asked what I wanted.**
4. **She asked why I was late.**
5. **He asked when I would arrive.**

### do/does/did تختفي لما تكون مجرد أدوات سؤال
**Where do you work?**  
→ **She asked where I worked.**

**What did he say?**  
→ **She asked what he had said.**

### Backshift
لو فعل النقل في الماضي، غالبًا الزمن يرجع خطوة:

**"What are you doing?"**  
→ **He asked what I was doing.**

**"Have you finished?"**  
→ **He asked if I had finished.**

**"Will you come?"**  
→ **He asked if I would come.**

**"Can you help?"**  
→ **He asked if I could help.**

# 11. Passive Voice in Tenses

## الفكرة
Active:
**Someone cleaned the room.**

Passive:
**The room was cleaned.**

في Passive بنركز على الشيء الذي وقع عليه الفعل، ومش لازم نذكر الفاعل.

القاعدة العامة:
`BE + V3`

والـ **BE** هو اللي بيتغير حسب الزمن.

## Present Simple Passive
`am/is/are + V3`

1. **English is spoken all over the world.**
2. **The reports are checked every day.**
3. **The system is updated regularly.**
4. **The emails are sent automatically.**
5. **This product is made in Italy.**

## Past Simple Passive
`was/were + V3`

1. **The car was repaired yesterday.**
2. **The building was built in 1990.**
3. **The email was sent this morning.**
4. **The problem was fixed yesterday.**
5. **The files were deleted by mistake.**

## Present Continuous Passive
`am/is/are + being + V3`

1. **The house is being painted.**
2. **The system is being tested.**
3. **The documents are being reviewed.**
4. **The road is being repaired.**
5. **The application is being updated.**

## Past Continuous Passive
`was/were + being + V3`

1. **The car was being repaired.**
2. **The room was being cleaned.**
3. **The system was being tested.**
4. **The documents were being prepared.**
5. **The road was being repaired.**

## Present Perfect Passive
`has/have + been + V3`

1. **The work has been completed.**
2. **The problem has been fixed.**
3. **The documents have been sent.**
4. **The system has been updated.**
5. **The project has been approved.**

## Past Perfect Passive
`had + been + V3`

1. **The work had been completed before we arrived.**
2. **The room had been cleaned.**
3. **The report had been sent before the meeting.**
4. **The problem had been fixed before the customer called.**
5. **The files had been deleted before we checked them.**

## Future Simple Passive
`will + be + V3`

1. **The work will be completed tomorrow.**
2. **The results will be announced soon.**
3. **The project will be finished next week.**
4. **The package will be delivered tomorrow.**
5. **The system will be updated tonight.**

## Going to Passive
`am/is/are going to be + V3`

- **The building is going to be demolished.**
- **The system is going to be upgraded.**
- **The road is going to be repaired.**
- **The project is going to be launched next month.**
- **The old system is going to be replaced.**

## Modal Passive
`modal + be + V3`

- **The problem must be fixed.**
- **The report should be completed today.**
- **The application can be installed easily.**
- **The rules must be followed.**
- **The information should be kept confidential.**

## Modal Perfect Passive
`modal + have been + V3`

- **The problem should have been fixed earlier.**
- **The email may have been sent by mistake.**
- **The file might have been deleted.**
- **The work could have been completed yesterday.**
- **The system must have been damaged.**

# 12. GET — شرح كامل

`get` من أكثر الكلمات مرونة في الإنجليزية، فلا تحفظ لها ترجمة واحدة.

## Get = receive
1. **I got an email.**
2. **I got a message.**
3. **She got a letter.**
4. **We got an email.**
5. **Did you get my message?**

= استلم/وصل له.

## Get = become
1. **I'm getting tired.**
2. **It's getting cold.**
3. **He's getting angry.**
4. **She's getting better.**
5. **It's getting late.**

= يبقى/يتحول لحالة.

## Get = arrive/reach
1. **What time did you get home?**
2. **I got home at eight.**
3. **We got to the office early.**
4. **When did you get there?**
5. **Did you get home safely?**

مهم:
**get home** بدون `to`.
لكن:
**get to work / get to the office / get to school**.

## Get = obtain/buy
1. **I need to get some food.**
2. **I need to get a new phone.**
3. **Where did you get that shirt?**
4. **I got a new job.**
5. **We need to get some information.**

## Get + adjective
**get tired / get angry / get sick / get better / get worse**

## Get Passive
في informal English ممكن `get + V3` بدل `be + V3`.

1. **My phone got stolen.**
2. **He got hurt.**
3. **The window got broken.**
4. **She got promoted.**
5. **I got invited to the party.**

## Get + person + to V
**I got him to help me.**
= قدرت أخليه يساعدني.

## Get + thing + V3
**I got my car repaired.**
= دبرت إن عربيتي تتصلح.

## Get + V-ing
- **Let's get started.**
- **I need to get going.**

## أشهر Phrasal Verbs
- get up = يقوم
- get in = يدخل
- get out = يخرج
- get back = يرجع
- get away = يبعد/يهرب
- get along = يتفاهم
- get over = يتخطى
- get through = يعدي/يتمكن من الوصول
- get on = يركب/يتقدم
- get off = ينزل

# 13. Passive vs Causative vs Get Passive

### Passive
**My car was repaired.**
= عربيتي اتصلحت. مجرد وصف.

### Causative
**I had my car repaired.**
= أنا رتبت إن حد يصلحها.

### Get Causative
**I got my car repaired.**
= دبرت/خليت حد يصلحها.

### Get Passive
**My car got damaged.**
= عربيتي اتضررت. وصف غير رسمي لما حصل.

# 14. قاعدة ذهبية سريعة

### Question Word + Infinitive
**what to do / where to go / how to fix it**

### Modal Perfect
- **must have V3** = أكيد تقريبًا
- **can't have V3** = مستحيل تقريبًا
- **may/might have V3** = يمكن
- **could have V3** = كان ممكن
- **should have V3** = كان المفروض
- **shouldn't have V3** = مكنش المفروض
- **would have V3** = نتيجة شرط ماضي
- **needn't have V3** = عملت حاجة مش ضرورية

### Causative
- `make + person + V`
- `have + person + V`
- `get + person + to V`

### Future Time Clause
`when/after/before/as soon as/once/until + Present Simple`

### Speak
لغة / رسمي / مخاطبة.

### Talk
محادثة / نقاش.

### Say
الكلام نفسه.

### Tell
الشخص الذي يتلقى الكلام.

### Passive
`BE + V3`

### Get
receive / become / arrive / obtain / cause / passive informal — حسب السياق.

# 17. طريقة التفكير الصح

لما تشوف تركيب جديد، متسألش نفسك الأول: "اسم القاعدة إيه؟"

اسأل:

**أنا عايز أوصل أي معنى؟**

- "مش عارف أعمل إيه" → **what to do**
- "أكيد حصل" → **must have + V3**
- "كان المفروض" → **should have + V3**
- "خليت شخص يعمل" → **make/have/get**
- "هعمل حاجة لما حاجة مستقبلية تحصل" → **when + Present Simple**
- "بتكلم عن اللغة" → **speak**
- "بتتكلم/بتناقش" → **talk**
- "بنقل كلام" → **say/tell**
- "الفعل أهم من الفاعل" → **Passive**
- "خليت حد يعمل خدمة لي" → **have/get + object + V3**
- "حاجة حصلت بشكل informal" → **get passive**

وده هو المستوى اللي يخليك تستخدم القاعدة في الكلام، بدل ما تحفظها في الامتحان بس.
