Describe a memorable sports match you watched.

Why do you think sportsmanship is important?

How has technology changed sports?

Do you think doping should be more heavily punished? Why?
Yes, I believe doping should carry stricter penalties because it undermines fair competition and puts pressure on clean athletes to cheat as well.
How do you think youth sports culture affects children's development?
Youth sports can build discipline and teamwork, but excessive pressure from parents or coaches can lead to burnout and take the joy out of the game.
What role do sponsors play in modern sports?
Sponsors provide the funding that keeps leagues and athletes financially viable, but their influence can sometimes shift the focus toward marketing over the sport itself.
Discuss the impact of injuries on an athlete's career.
A serious injury can derail an athlete's career, forcing early retirement or a long, difficult recovery that affects their performance and confidence.
How do underdog stories affect fans' emotional connection to sports?
Underdog stories create a powerful emotional bond because fans root for the team that overcomes the odds, which makes victory feel more meaningful.

To what extent should sports organizations be held accountable for the mental health of athletes?
I'd argue organizations bear significant responsibility, since the intense scrutiny and pressure athletes face are direct products of the system; providing robust psychological support shouldn't be optional.
How has the commercialization of sports affected its original spirit of competition?
Commercialization has undeniably shifted priorities toward revenue and entertainment value, sometimes at the expense of grassroots development, though it has also expanded opportunities for athletes worldwide.
Discuss the ethical implications of genetic engineering in athletic performance.
Genetic engineering raises profound questions about fairness and what we consider a "natural" achievement, potentially creating an unbridgeable gap between enhanced and unenhanced athletes.
What's your view on the relationship between national identity and international sports competitions?
International competitions often become a proxy for national pride, which can unite people constructively but also fuel unhealthy rivalries that go beyond the sport itself.
How might AI and data analytics reshape coaching and strategy in the coming decade?
AI will likely give coaches unprecedented insight into player performance and opposition tendencies, though the human elements of leadership and motivation will remain irreplaceable.
